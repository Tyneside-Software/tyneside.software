## 1
After the joke lands, the world is still there, slightly rearranged, like sheets.

## 2
Not a paradox: punchlines do not delete rooms; they reveal which walls were paper.

## 3
Amen, then — which means *so be it*, not *so stop thinking*. Laugh. Breathe. Funny old world.
