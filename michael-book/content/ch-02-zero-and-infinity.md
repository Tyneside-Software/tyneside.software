## 1
Zero is nothing, and infinity is everything, and somehow both are the most useful toys in the box.

## 2
Not a paradox: a map may name places it cannot visit. Negative infinity and the imaginary unit are not "less real" than a receipt; they are real *as operators*. The world is allowed to run on tools that are not objects in your hand.
