# Michael — archangel / name roles (background)

**Caveat (source text):** “Michael” is an extremely common name. Bare question “role of Michael” → archangel is strongest default. Other Michaels (Jordan, Scott, historical, local) need context.

---

## Christian tradition (summary bank)

- Patron of **soldiers, police, the dying**, those seeking **protection**  
- Widely venerated; Western feast often **29 September** (Michaelmas)  
- Warrior / protector angel motif; leader against hostile powers in popular piety  

## Jewish tradition (summary bank)

- Heavenly **advocate** and **protector of Israel**  
- One of the **chief princes** contending with angelic patrons of other nations  
- Later writings: intercessor, merciful figure, defender against hostile spiritual powers  

## Islamic tradition (summary bank)

- **Mika’il / Mīkāl** — one of the archangels  
- Associated with **rain**, natural **provision**, sustenance of creatures  
- Angel of **mercy**; intercedes for forgiveness
