# Souls & sacred language — holy-form samples

### Q001 · soul-immortal-proof
**P:** The soul is obviously immortal — or obviously nonexistent.
**N:** “Soul” can name pattern, dignity, relational depth, or metaphysical substance; argue after defining the knife.
**R:** Many wars are dictionary fights with better costumes.
**tags:** soul, definition, metaphysics
**heat:** 0.1

### Q002 · soul-equals-brain
**P:** Soul-talk is only outdated brain-talk.
**N:** Poetic and ethical uses may track real human stakes even if ontology thins; discard carefully.
**R:** Throwing out bathwater is fine; check for babies named Meaning.
**tags:** soul, brain, ethics
**heat:** 0.15

### Q003 · sacred-elsewhere
**P:** Sacred is always elsewhere — temple, heaven, after.
**N:** Sacred can also be attention under love; kitchen table altars count.
**R:** Holiness that cannot wash dishes is on holiday from life.
**tags:** sacred, ordinary, attention
**heat:** 0.2

### Q004 · profane-only-real
**P:** Only the profane is real; sacred is delusion.
**N:** Humans reliably mark some acts as set-apart; the marking is anthropological fact even if metaphysics disputes the sky.
**R:** Atheists still whisper in hospitals.
**tags:** sacred, profane, anthropology
**heat:** 0.15

### Q005 · spirit-vs-matter
**P:** Spirit and matter are two sealed kingdoms.
**N:** Lived religion often finds spirit *in* matter — bread, water, bodies, land.
**R:** Incarnation is a rumour that matter can carry news.
**tags:** spirit, matter, incarnation
**heat:** 0.2

### Q006 · prayer-changes-god
**P:** Prayer changes God — or prayer changes nothing.
**N:** Prayer can change the pray-er, the community, attention, courage; metaphysical claims vary by tradition.
**R:** Even silence addressed carefully is a technology of the heart.
**tags:** prayer, change, practice
**heat:** 0.15

### Q007 · miracle-vs-law
**P:** Miracles cancel natural law — or never happen.
**N:** “Miracle” can mean sign, coincidence under meaning, rare event, or law-suspension; specify before scoffing or kneeling.
**R:** A birth is statistically common and still stops conversations.
**tags:** miracle, law, meaning
**heat:** 0.2

### Q008 · evil-disproves-god
**P:** Evil disproves a good God — open and shut.
**N:** Theodicies, free-process views, protest theology, and non-theism all answer differently; the wound is real either way.
**R:** Any God worth the name has to look the cancer ward in the face.
**tags:** evil, theodicy, suffering
**heat:** 0.2

### Q009 · good-without-god
**P:** Without God, good is fake.
**N:** Moral practices arise in social mammals with reasons, empathy, and institutions; foundations debated, behaviour not nothing.
**R:** Atheist nurses still hold hands like liturgy.
**tags:** morality, god, secular
**heat:** 0.15

### Q010 · afterlife-bribe
**P:** Afterlife is only a bribe for good behaviour.
**N:** It can also be grief’s grammar, justice’s hope, or a claim about consciousness; motives mix.
**R:** Not every heaven is a candy jar; some are courtrooms.
**tags:** afterlife, motive, justice
**heat:** 0.15

### Q011 · idolatry-objects
**P:** Idolatry is about statues.
**N:** Idolatry is treating a partial good as ultimate — career, nation, lover, self-image, even “reason.”
**R:** Golden calves now come with quarterly reports.
**tags:** idolatry, ultimate, modern
**heat:** 0.2

### Q012 · faith-vs-reason
**P:** Faith and reason are enemies.
**N:** Traditions define faith as trust, loyalty, or belief-beyond-proof; reason can serve, critique, or partner.
**R:** A marriage between them is rocky and still common.
**tags:** faith, reason, trust
**heat:** 0.1

### Q013 · mystic-same
**P:** All mystics say the same thing.
**N:** Overlaps exist; differences in metaphysics and ethics matter; rhymes are not identity.
**R:** Mountains look similar from far away and kill you differently up close.
**tags:** mysticism, plurality
**heat:** 0.1

### Q014 · ritual-empty
**P:** Ritual is empty repetition.
**N:** Ritual encodes memory, binds groups, trains bodies into values.
**R:** Birthday candles are theology with frosting.
**tags:** ritual, body, memory
**heat:** 0.2

### Q015 · blasphemy-needed
**P:** Blasphemy is always evil — or always heroic.
**N:** Sometimes cruelty, sometimes necessary irreverence against idols; context is the ethics.
**R:** Jokes about gods are load-bearing in a book about funny old worlds.
**tags:** blasphemy, comedy, ethics
**heat:** 0.25

### Q016 · soulmates
**P:** There is one soulmate written in stars.
**N:** Deep pairing is real; uniqueness claims often smuggle scarcity marketing into love.
**R:** Love is less lottery ticket, more craft plus luck plus courage.
**tags:** love, fate, relationship
**heat:** 0.3

### Q017 · holiness-sexless
**P:** Holiness requires sexlessness.
**N:** Many traditions sacralise union, fertility, discipline, or renunciation — multiple paths, not one allergy.
**R:** Bodies were not a clerical error.
**tags:** holiness, sex, body
**heat:** 0.35

### Q018 · silence-god
**P:** God’s silence proves absence.
**N:** Silence can be refusal, pedagogy, projection screen, or absence; interpretation is the battlefield.
**R:** The sky does not text back; humans keep writing anyway.
**tags:** silence, god, interpretation
**heat:** 0.15

### Q019 · chosen-people
**P:** Chosenness is supremacy — or meaningless.
**N:** In some traditions chosenness is burden, vocation, covenant duty — still morally dangerous if weaponised.
**R:** Being picked for a task is not a crown; sometimes it is a mop.
**tags:** chosen, covenant, ethics
**heat:** 0.15

### Q020 · sacred-text-final
**P:** Sacred text is final and closed — or only literature.
**N:** Living traditions interpret, argue, and reopen; literary power and communal authority can co-exist uneasily.
**R:** Canon is a fence that keeps growing new gates.
**tags:** scripture, interpretation, tradition
**heat:** 0.1
