# Vertical / horizontal cross — source bank

## Popular Christian framing (widespread, not high academic only)

**Core idea is not Master’s invention** — extremely common in preaching/blogs:

- **Vertical beam** = relationship with **God** (upward: faith, prayer, accountability to the divine).  
- **Horizontal beam** = relationship with **other people / society / community** (neighbour, social ethics, interpersonal life).  

Typical formula: *“The vertical beam symbolizes your relationship with God while the horizontal beam represents your relationship with others. You need both to form a cross.”*

= Authentic faith must hold **both** dimensions; without both you do not have a complete cross.  
Popular theology across **Protestant, Catholic, evangelical** circles.

**Master reaction:** “lol, apparently the church have realised this too :)”
