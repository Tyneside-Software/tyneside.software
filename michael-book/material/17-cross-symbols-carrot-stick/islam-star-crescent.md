## Quick factual bank (for later precision pass)

- **Star and crescent** is widely used as a **cultural / national / historical** emblem associated with many Muslim-majority contexts; it is **not** a universal doctrinal “logo of Islam” equivalent to the cross in Christianity.  
- **Aniconism:** strong traditions against figural images of God / often of prophets; practice varies by school, era, region. Geometry, calligraphy, arabesque flourish where figurative art is restricted.  
- So Master’s double hunch is **both partially right**: moon-star is *common association* **and** “symbols are banned” tracks *aniconic pressure* without being a simple total ban on all symbols.
