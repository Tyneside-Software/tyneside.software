3. **Existence is eternal.** United, no outside → no coming-into / passing-out that requires non-existence. No temporal edge of the whole.

4. **Existence is all-encompassing.** Any limit needs a beyond or limiter that would already exist inside the all.

5. **Consciousness is fundamental, not emergent.** Axiom is experiential; reversing order (matter first, experience later) explains the datum by what is only known through the datum.

6. **Existence prefers this moment.** Unlimited, no reason to withhold; past/future as modalities of undivided field. Ontological preference, not mere psychology.

7. **True separation is impossible.** Subject/object, self/other, sacred/profane = internal articulations. Ultimate monotheism (word “mono” already too numerical). Hell impossible — damned would stand outside the only thing that is.
