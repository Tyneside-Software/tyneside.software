What is the role of MichaelThe most common interpretation of “the role of Michael,” given no further context, is Archangel Michael (also called Saint Michael or Mika’il), a prominent figure in Judaism, Christianity, and Islam.
His name is Hebrew for “Who is like God?” (a rhetorical question affirming that no one equals God).
Biblical roles
Michael appears by name mainly in three places:

In the Book of Daniel he is called “one of the chief princes” who helps another angel overcome opposition from the “prince of Persia,” and “the great prince who protects your people” (Israel). He stands as the heavenly guardian and champion of God’s people in times of distress.
In Jude 1:9 he is explicitly titled “the archangel.” He disputes with the devil over the body of Moses but does not pronounce judgment himself, saying instead “The Lord rebuke you.”
In Revelation 12:7-9 he leads the angels in a war in heaven against the dragon (identified as Satan/the devil) and his angels, casting them down to the earth.

Overall, Scripture portrays him as a high-ranking warrior-protector who fights spiritual forces of evil and defends God’s people.
In Christian tradition (especially Catholic)
Catholic teaching traditionally assigns him four main roles:

Leader of the Army of God / celestial forces who triumphs over the powers of Hell.
Angel of death who assists and guides the souls of the faithful at the hour of death.
Weigher of souls (often depicted holding scales) at the Last Judgment.
Guardian and protector of the Church.

He is the patron of soldiers, police, the dying, and those seeking protection, and is widely venerated (feast day 29 September in the Western Church).
In Jewish tradition
He is the heavenly advocate and protector of Israel, one of the chief princes who contends with the angelic patrons of other nations. Later writings expand his role as intercessor, merciful figure, and defender against hostile spiritual powers.
In Islam
Known as Mika’il (or Mīkāl), he is one of the archangels. He is associated with rain, natural provision, and the sustenance of creatures; he is also regarded as an angel of mercy who intercedes for forgiveness.
