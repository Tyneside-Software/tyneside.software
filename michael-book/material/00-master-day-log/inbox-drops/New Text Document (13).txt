Find the most appropriate Bible verseRomans 4:17
“...the God who gives life to the dead and calls into being things that were not.”
(NIV; similarly ESV: “who gives life to the dead and calls into existence the things that do not exist.”)
This is the strongest single match for the chat.

“Gives life to the dead” lines up directly with “My phone came back to life.”
“Calls into being things that were not” / “calls into existence the things that do not exist” is a near-perfect biblical parallel for “manifest one” (Gabriel won’t need to bring a new phone into existence).
The rest of the exchange—the resigned “no point… when you already know how it’s going to go” and the mild disappointment about the iPhone—fits naturally under the same sovereign God whose purpose prevails over our plans.

(Runner-ups that also fit parts of the chat well include Isaiah 46:10 for the “already know how it’s going to go” line, and Proverbs 19:21 for plans versus the Lord’s purpose, but Romans 4:17 uniquely captures both the resurrection of the phone and the “manifest” idea in one verse.)
