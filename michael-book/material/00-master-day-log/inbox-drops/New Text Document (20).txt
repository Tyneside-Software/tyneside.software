give me the axiomatic chain put simply behind Joshua Thomson's book Existence ThereforeThe axiomatic chain in Joshua Thomson’s Experience, Therefore (the book’s actual title; it is often loosely referred to via its core claim about existence) is presented by the author as a short, unbroken logical sequence starting from one undeniable starting point.
Starting axiom
The only thing you can be objectively certain of is: Your experience of this moment exists.
From that single axiom (what Thomson calls the basis of “Experiential Axiomatism”), the chain runs as follows (paraphrased and cleaned for clarity from the book’s own summary):

Non-existence is the only alternative to existence.
The impossibility of nothing is the inevitability of everything.
Therefore, existence is united in its defiance of the opposite. (Any disunity would require an impossible boundary.)
Therefore, existence is eternal. (Energy cannot be created or destroyed.)
Therefore, existence is all-encompassing. (Any limit or shortcoming would require an existing gatekeeper.)
Therefore, consciousness is fundamental, not emergent.
Therefore, existence prefers this moment — unlimited and unbound.
Therefore, true separation is impossible.
Only ultimate monotheism remains.
There is only existence.

Endpoint of the chain
Thomson concludes that this forces the existence of God (as the unified, eternal, all-encompassing, conscious reality itself) and, correspondingly, the impossibility of Hell. The framework is offered as pure reason with no faith or dogma required, intended to dissolve the usual religion-vs-atheism standoff.
That is the simplified axiomatic chain exactly as the author presents it.
