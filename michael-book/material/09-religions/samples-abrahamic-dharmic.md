# Religions — Abrahamic & Dharmic streams — holy-form samples

*Steelmanning patterns, not converting Master to any altar.*

### Q001 · monotheism-many-names
**P:** If God is one, many religions prove fraud.
**N:** One reality can wear many names and local weather; fraud exists, but plurality alone is not proof of zero.
**R:** The moon does not multiply when languages do.
**tags:** monotheism, plurality, names
**heat:** 0.1

### Q002 · judaism-law-love
**P:** Law kills spirit — or law is all.
**N:** Jewish tradition holds law as love’s architecture and argues endlessly inside it; argument is devotion.
**R:** A people who wrestle angels invent theology as martial art.
**tags:** judaism, law, argument
**heat:** 0.15

### Q003 · christianity-death-life
**P:** A dead god cannot save — or only death saves.
**N:** Crucifixion/resurrection pattern claims life through loss; paradox is the brand, for better and worse.
**R:** Christianity’s logo is an execution device wearing jewellery.
**tags:** christianity, cross, paradox
**heat:** 0.2

### Q004 · christianity-exclusive
**P:** “Only way” is either pure arrogance or pure necessity.
**N:** Traditions claim uniqueness from inside covenant logic; ethics of the claim need separate audit from the poetry of devotion.
**R:** Exclusive doors still have hinges that history oil-cans.
**tags:** christianity, exclusivism, covenant
**heat:** 0.15

### Q005 · islam-submission-freedom
**P:** Submission cancels freedom.
**N:** Islam names peace through aligning with reality’s grain; freedom-as-whim and freedom-as-right-relation differ.
**R:** A musician “submits” to tempo and becomes freer in song.
**tags:** islam, submission, freedom
**heat:** 0.15

### Q006 · islam-tawhid
**P:** Absolute oneness leaves no room for the world.
**N:** Tawhid can mean reality’s unity without erasing creation’s signs; world as ayah (sign) not rival god.
**R:** Multiplicity as handwriting, not second author.
**tags:** islam, tawhid, signs
**heat:** 0.1

### Q007 · hinduism-many-gods
**P:** Many gods contradict ultimate unity.
**N:** Streams hold many forms as faces of Brahman or distinct powers; both& is native, not a Western patch.
**R:** One ocean, many temples on the shore.
**tags:** hinduism, polytheism, brahman
**heat:** 0.15

### Q008 · hinduism-maya
**P:** If world is maya (illusion), ethics collapse.
**N:** Maya can mean mis-taking appearance for ultimate; relative world still binds karma and care.
**R:** Dream-debts still collect in the morning if you kicked someone.
**tags:** hinduism, maya, ethics
**heat:** 0.15

### Q009 · buddhism-no-self
**P:** No-self means nobody suffers and nothing matters.
**N:** Anatta critiques permanent essence, not the reality of pain processes; compassion intensifies when clinging loosens.
**R:** Empty of fixed self, full of neighbours.
**tags:** buddhism, anatta, compassion
**heat:** 0.15

### Q010 · buddhism-desire
**P:** End desire and you end life.
**N:** Critique targets tanha (craving/clinging), not all motivation; skillful desire toward awakening is named differently.
**R:** The thirst that kills and the thirst that waters are not the same drink.
**tags:** buddhism, desire, tanha
**heat:** 0.2

### Q011 · sikh-one-and-many
**P:** One God plus saintly lineage is sneaky polytheism.
**N:** Sikh emphasis on Ik Onkar with human gurus as guides, not rival deities; form serves the Formless.
**R:** A lamp is not the sun and still helps at night.
**tags:** sikh, oneness, guru
**heat:** 0.1

### Q012 · jain-ahimsa-absolute
**P:** Absolute non-harm is impossible, so ahimsa is fake.
**N:** Jain practice is graded rigor and aspiration; impossibility of perfection does not cancel direction.
**R:** You cannot be perfectly harmless; you can be less of a hurricane.
**tags:** jain, ahimsa, ethics
**heat:** 0.15

### Q013 · karma-blame
**P:** Karma means blaming victims.
**N:** Crude karma-police is real and ugly; sophisticated accounts include conditions, intention, and collective webs.
**R:** “They deserved it” is often laziness wearing incense.
**tags:** karma, justice, misuse
**heat:** 0.2

### Q014 · reincarnation-vs-once
**P:** One life or many — only one can be true, so the other camp is stupid.
**N:** Both are existential frames that shape ethics of urgency and patience; truth-claim remains open, function differs.
**R:** Live as if rare and as if responsible beyond one CV.
**tags:** reincarnation, mortality, ethics
**heat:** 0.15

### Q015 · atheism-religion
**P:** Atheism is just another religion — or nothing like one.
**N:** Atheism can be thin denial or thick worldview with rituals of identity; analogy holds in patches.
**R:** Some non-gods are worshipped with remarkable regularity.
**tags:** atheism, worldview, ritual
**heat:** 0.15

### Q016 · agnostic-coward
**P:** Agnosticism is cowardice.
**N:** It can be epistemic humility under incomplete evidence; courage is sometimes not faking certainty.
**R:** “I do not know” is a full sentence, not a shrug emoji.
**tags:** agnosticism, humility, knowledge
**heat:** 0.1

### Q017 · covenant-contract
**P:** Covenant is just a contract with sky-lawyer.
**N:** Covenant binds identity and ethics beyond spot-market deals; thicker than invoice, riskier than hobby.
**R:** Some signatures rearrange a people’s calendar forever.
**tags:** covenant, identity, law
**heat:** 0.15

### Q018 · idol-image
**P:** Images are always idolatry — or never.
**N:** Traditions split on whether images aid devotion or trap it; the ethics is about ultimate vs aid.
**R:** A picture of your child is not your child — and you still kiss it.
**tags:** image, idolatry, devotion
**heat:** 0.15

### Q019 · scripture-violence
**P:** Scriptures command violence, so religions are violence.
**N:** Texts contain war, peace, ban, and mercy; communities select, interpret, restrain, or inflame.
**R:** A library is not guilty of every book the same way a reader is.
**tags:** scripture, violence, interpretation
**heat:** 0.2

### Q020 · mystics-agree
**P:** Mystics of all faiths prove one religion underneath.
**N:** Shared techniques of silence and love appear; doctrines still diverge on self, God, world.
**R:** Similar hiking gear does not make all mountains one mountain.
**tags:** mysticism, unity, difference
**heat:** 0.1
