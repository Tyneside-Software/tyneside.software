# Duality & form — holy-form samples

### Q001 · light-dark-good-evil
**P:** Light is good and dark is evil.
**N:** Dark is also rest, soil, pupil, womb, privacy, and the condition that makes stars visible; light is also desert, bleach, interrogation lamp, and migraine.
**R:** Moral cartoons fit children’s rooms and armies; adults need a dimmer switch, not a sword.
**tags:** light, dark, morality, both&
**heat:** 0.1

**P:** Up is better than down.
**N:** Roots go down to feed the crown; graves go down so gardens can rise; “down” is where foundations hide.
**R:** Heaven-talk that never digs is just altitude snobbery.
**tags:** up, down, hierarchy, earth
**heat:** 0.1
