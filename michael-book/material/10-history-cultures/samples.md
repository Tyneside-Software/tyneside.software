# History & cultures — holy-form samples

### Q001 · progress-real
**P:** History is progress.
**N:** Some measures improve (medicine, rights expansions) while horrors innovate too; progress is local and reversible.
**R:** Antibiotics and concentration camps share a century — update your graphs.
**tags:** progress, modern, dual
**heat:** 0.15

### Q002 · history-wheel
**P:** History is only a wheel — nothing new.
**N:** Patterns rhyme; tech and scale change the rhyme scheme; “only wheel” erases novelty’s teeth.
**R:** Empires rhyme; smartphones are a new chorus.
**tags:** cycle, novelty, empire
**heat:** 0.15

### Q003 · great-man
**P:** History is made by great individuals.
**N:** Structures, masses, accidents, and leaders co-author; “great man” is spotlight bias.
**R:** Behind every statue is a supply chain and a weather report.
**tags:** great-man, structure, bias
**heat:** 0.1

### Q004 · no-individuals
**P:** Individuals do not matter — only systems.
**N:** Systems are enacted by persons; tipping points often have names and stubbornness.
**R:** A system without protagonists is a spreadsheet cosplaying as tragedy.
**tags:** individual, system
**heat:** 0.1

### Q005 · empire-order
**P:** Empire is only order and peace.
**N:** Empires export roads and extract blood; both can be true in one ledger.
**R:** Pax is often quieter for the capital than the frontier.
**tags:** empire, peace, violence
**heat:** 0.2

### Q006 · empire-only-evil
**P:** Empire is only evil.
**N:** Critique stands; still map law, trade, hybrid cultures honestly or propaganda wins.
**R:** Moral clarity without facts becomes a different empire of story.
**tags:** empire, critique, honesty
**heat:** 0.15

### Q007 · civilisation-barbarism
**P:** Civilisation versus barbarism is a clean line.
**N:** The line is usually drawn by the side with better press and worse memory.
**R:** Everyone else’s dinner looks like savagery until it becomes brunch.
**tags:** civilisation, othering
**heat:** 0.2

### Q008 · culture-pure
**P:** Cultures are pure essences.
**N:** Cultures hybridise, steal, trade, conquer, marry; purity is a political costume.
**R:** Your “authentic recipe” has a passport full of stamps.
**tags:** culture, hybrid, purity
**heat:** 0.15

### Q009 · tradition-vs-change
**P:** Tradition forbids change — or change kills tradition.
**N:** Living tradition is curated change; dead tradition is a museum with a gift shop.
**R:** The most traditional act is sometimes inventing what the ancestors would have needed.
**tags:** tradition, change
**heat:** 0.15

### Q010 · revolution-clean
**P:** Revolution cleans the slate.
**N:** Revolutions inherit institutions, grudges, and human nature; slates smear.
**R:** Year Zero still has last year’s plumbing.
**tags:** revolution, continuity
**heat:** 0.15

### Q011 · memory-history
**P:** History is just memory written down.
**N:** History is argued reconstruction with sources; memory is personal and political too — cousins, not twins.
**R:** Archives are haunted by what nobody filed.
**tags:** history, memory, archive
**heat:** 0.1

### Q012 · winners-write
**P:** Winners write history — end of story.
**N:** Often true; also losers, exiles, archaeologists, and later revolutions rewrite; struggle continues.
**R:** The graveyard keeps submitting footnotes.
**tags:** winners, narrative, power
**heat:** 0.15

### Q013 · golden-age
**P:** There was a golden age we must return to.
**N:** Golden ages are edited highlight reels; return fantasies skip the disease and the hierarchy.
**R:** Nostalgia is a filter with better skin.
**tags:** golden-age, nostalgia
**heat:** 0.15

### Q014 · technology-neutral
**P:** Technology is neutral; only users are good/bad.
**N:** Design embeds values; affordances steer; neutrality is a marketing claim.
**R:** A gun and a plough are both “tools” and not the same sermon.
**tags:** technology, values, design
**heat:** 0.15

### Q015 · nation-natural
**P:** Nations are natural kinds.
**N:** Nations are imagined communities with real armies; invented does not mean unreal.
**R:** Borders are sharpie lines that learned to kill.
**tags:** nation, imagination, real
**heat:** 0.2

### Q016 · colonialism-past
**P:** Colonialism is finished history.
**N:** Formal empires fell; patterns of extraction, language, border, and trauma persist.
**R:** The past is not even past — it is in the outlet adapter.
**tags:** colonialism, afterlife, present
**heat:** 0.2

### Q017 · slavery-one-story
**P:** Slavery is one people’s unique sin — or everyone’s equal shrug.
**N:** Near-universal historical horror with distinct modern racialised forms; specificity and breadth both required.
**R:** Compare carefully; do not erase; do not relativise into fog.
**tags:** slavery, ethics, history
**heat:** 0.2

### Q018 · war-human-nature
**P:** War proves human nature is violence.
**N:** Humans also trade, nurse, and compose; capacity ≠ destiny.
**R:** A species that only warred would not have invented the lullaby.
**tags:** war, human-nature, dual
**heat:** 0.2

### Q019 · peace-boring
**P:** Peace is boring; war is meaningful.
**N:** Peace is complex labour; war steals meaning by force and sells it as glory.
**R:** Boredom is sometimes what justice feels like after the drums stop.
**tags:** peace, war, meaning
**heat:** 0.2

### Q020 · city-corrupt
**P:** Cities corrupt; rural is pure.
**N:** Cities concentrate vice and art and medicine; rural holds care and constraint and also cruelty.
**R:** Pastoral cosplay forgets the gossip and the isolation.
**tags:** city, rural, purity
**heat:** 0.15

### Q021 · oral-vs-written-cultures
**P:** Written cultures are advanced; oral are behind.
**N:** Different memory technologies; each loses what the other keeps.
**R:** A statute book and an epic singer are rival hard drives.
**tags:** oral, written, culture
**heat:** 0.1

### Q022 · trade-peace
**P:** Trade brings peace.
**N:** Trade can bind or exploit; interdependence lowers some wars and funds others.
**R:** Merchants hate instability and love a profitable border.
**tags:** trade, peace, money
**heat:** 0.15

### Q023 · art-luxury
**P:** Art is luxury after survival.
**N:** Humans make beauty under siege; art is also survival of meaning.
**R:** Cave hands were not painted after the mortgage cleared.
**tags:** art, survival, meaning
**heat:** 0.2

### Q024 · museum-neutral
**P:** Museums are neutral education.
**N:** They are also trophies, contexts stripped, stories framed by curators and donors.
**R:** Every glass case asks: whose shelf, whose loss?
**tags:** museum, power, story
**heat:** 0.15

### Q025 · future-determined
**P:** History’s arc is determined.
**N:** Trends exist; contingency is real; prophecy is often politics in a robe.
**R:** The future is a committee meeting that has not started.
**tags:** future, contingency, arc
**heat:** 0.1
