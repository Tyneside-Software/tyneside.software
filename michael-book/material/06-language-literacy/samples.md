# Language & literacy — holy-form samples

### Q001 · name-is-thing
**P:** The name is the thing.
**N:** Names point, compress, summon attention; the map is not the mountain, but a good map moves feet.
**R:** Call a rose something else and the thorn still files a complaint.
**tags:** naming, map, reference
**heat:** 0.1

### Q002 · name-is-nothing
**P:** Names are arbitrary, so language is empty.
**N:** Arbitrary origin ≠ empty function; conventions coordinate armies, love, and science.
**R:** “Arbitrary” still stops traffic when painted on a sign.
**tags:** convention, power, language
**heat:** 0.1

### Q003 · cut-and-join
**P:** To name is only to separate.
**N:** Naming also links into categories, stories, and search; scissors and thread in one gesture.
**R:** Dictionary is a city of bridges pretending to be walls.
**tags:** category, link, cut
**heat:** 0.15

### Q004 · babel-curse
**P:** Many languages are a curse of confusion.
**N:** Many languages are many toolkits; translation is hard because worlds differ, not only words.
**R:** Monolingual certainty is a padded room with excellent acoustics.
**tags:** translation, plurality, babel
**heat:** 0.15

### Q005 · translation-perfect
**P:** Perfect translation is possible if you are careful.
**N:** Connotation, culture, music of phrase leak; fidelity is a negotiation.
**R:** Every translation is a cover song that hopes the crowd still dances.
**tags:** translation, fidelity, art
**heat:** 0.15

### Q006 · literal-only
**P:** Only literal language tells truth; metaphor lies.
**N:** Metaphor structures thought; science is thick with models that are metaphors with teeth.
**R:** “Particle” was never a tiny ball with a birth certificate.
**tags:** metaphor, science, truth
**heat:** 0.1

### Q007 · metaphor-only
**P:** Everything is metaphor, so nothing is solid.
**N:** Some statements constrain prediction harder than others; solidity is graded, not binary.
**R:** You still should not test gravity as “just a metaphor” from a roof.
**tags:** metaphor, constraint, realism
**heat:** 0.1

### Q008 · english-neutral
**P:** English is a neutral medium.
**N:** Empire, class, prestige, and loanword history ride inside it; every tongue has politics.
**R:** Spelling is a fossil bed of invasions.
**tags:** english, power, history
**heat:** 0.15

### Q009 · literacy-civilised
**P:** Literacy makes people civilised; orality is primitive.
**N:** Oral cultures carry law, epic, science-of-place; literacy rewires and also forgets.
**R:** A songline can out-navigate a bad PDF.
**tags:** literacy, orality, culture
**heat:** 0.15

### Q010 · word-magic
**P:** Words cannot change reality — magic is fake.
**N:** Speech acts marry, sentence, hire, declare war; attention steered is world steered.
**R:** “I do” is a spell with paperwork.
**tags:** speech-act, magic, law
**heat:** 0.2

### Q011 · silence-empty
**P:** Silence is empty of meaning.
**N:** Silence frames, refuses, consents, threatens, rests; music needs it.
**R:** The pause is a syllable of power.
**tags:** silence, music, power
**heat:** 0.2

### Q012 · grammar-prison
**P:** Grammar imprisons thought.
**N:** Grammar enables complex thought-sharing; constraints birth creativity (see sonnets, code).
**R:** Jazz knows chords and still misbehaves on purpose.
**tags:** grammar, constraint, creativity
**heat:** 0.15

### Q013 · dictionary-final
**P:** The dictionary settles meaning forever.
**N:** Dictionaries trail usage; living speech leads; entries are weather reports.
**R:** Words date; some sleep around.
**tags:** dictionary, usage, change
**heat:** 0.15

### Q014 · swear-low
**P:** Swear words are low and unholy.
**N:** Taboo language carries pain-relief, bonding, emphasis; sacred and profane share a nervous system.
**R:** A well-placed curse can be pastoral care.
**tags:** profanity, sacred, body
**heat:** 0.3

### Q015 · poetry-vs-code
**P:** Poetry is vague; code is precise — never the same craft.
**N:** Both compress, pattern, and fail beautifully when misread; precision serves different gods.
**R:** A bug is a metaphor that crashed production.
**tags:** poetry, code, precision
**heat:** 0.15

### Q016 · lying-language
**P:** Language exists to lie.
**N:** Language exists to coordinate; lying is a parasite on trust’s host.
**R:** Without mostly-truth, lies have no blood to drink.
**tags:** truth, lie, trust
**heat:** 0.15

### Q017 · private-language
**P:** My meanings are only mine.
**N:** Private sensation meets public words in rough marriage; understanding is approximate communion.
**R:** “You know what I mean” is the secret handshake of the species.
**tags:** meaning, private, communication
**heat:** 0.2

### Q018 · reading-passive
**P:** Reading is passive consumption.
**N:** Reading is co-performance; the mind simulates, predicts, rewrites.
**R:** A good book uses your neurons as unpaid actors.
**tags:** reading, mind, active
**heat:** 0.15

### Q019 · writing-preserves
**P:** Writing freezes thought perfectly.
**N:** Writing freezes a version; context rots; future readers remix.
**R:** Dead authors keep changing their minds through living eyes.
**tags:** writing, time, interpretation
**heat:** 0.15

### Q020 · pun-low-art
**P:** Puns are the lowest form of humour.
**N:** Puns expose language’s double doors; double meaning is the holy form’s cousin.
**R:** A pun is a paradox that wants a laugh track.
**tags:** pun, comedy, double
**heat:** 0.25

### Q021 · definition-trap
**P:** If we define terms, disagreement ends.
**N:** Definitions can hide smuggled values; progress needs shared use, not only shared glossary.
**R:** Debates often need better verbs, not longer nouns.
**tags:** definition, debate, values
**heat:** 0.1

### Q022 · cliche-dead
**P:** Clichés are dead language.
**N:** Clichés are successful compressions worn smooth; revive by angle or retire with honour.
**R:** Even “funny old world” was new the first time someone noticed.
**tags:** cliche, compression, comedy
**heat:** 0.2

### Q023 · pronoun-war
**P:** Pronouns are trivial / pronouns are everything.
**N:** Pronouns are small words that carry belonging and exclusion; trivial in grammar, heavy in politics of recognition.
**R:** Little keys open big doors; that is not a reason to swallow the keychain.
**tags:** pronouns, identity, politics
**heat:** 0.2

### Q024 · story-lie
**P:** Stories are lies.
**N:** Stories are structured simulations that can carry truths denser than bullet points.
**R:** The species that only listed facts went extinct of boredom (hypothetically, with feeling).
**tags:** story, truth, fiction
**heat:** 0.15

### Q025 · final-word
**P:** Someone will have the last word.
**N:** Conversation is ecological; last words are local weather.
**R:** This sentence is already being rewritten by your disagreement.
