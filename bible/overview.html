Overview · sixty-six books

The whole story

Sixty-six books, one long story: God makes a world, a people go wrong and are called, judged, and kept, and in the end the story opens onto a city where there is no more curse. This page is the map. Each book below is a door — what it is, in a few sentences — then you can open the text itself. Chapter-by-chapter notes come later.

Old Testament New Testament Library of books

Old Testament

The Old Testament is Israel’s scriptures: law, history, songs, and prophets. It runs from creation to a people waiting, after exile, for God to act again.

Law

The Torah — five books. How the world begins, how a family becomes a nation, and the covenant given at Sinai.

Beginnings

Genesis

50 chapters · 35,639 words

God speaks a world into being. Humans are made for it, then break it. Flood, a fresh start, then a family: Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph — promised a land and a people, ending in Egypt, waiting.

New Testament

The New Testament is the story of Jesus of Nazareth, the church that grew from him, letters that teach and correct that church, and a last vision of how the story ends.

Gospels

Four tellings of Jesus: who he is, what he did, how he died, and that he rose. Same Lord, four cameras.
