What it is

An online platform where practitioners offer live individual and (especially) group sessions that help people learn and practise skills to improve mental wellbeing and deal with stress.

Classes and workshops help people build a practical toolkit for wellbeing and integrate practices into daily life.

Market gap

A true one-stop shop for learning and practising wellbeing skills with live accountability, plus a library of recordings.

Especially for people who struggle to access support because of:

  • Cost
  • Busy lives
  • NHS waiting lists
  • Being housebound
  • Lack of childcare
  • Other access barriers

Possible session types

Anything deliverable by video, for example:

  • Therapy skills (not a substitute for clinical care)
  • Yoga
  • Mindfulness
  • Music lessons
  • Stress-management workshops

Pricing model

Membership — monthly and annual.

Tiers for:

  • Individuals
  • Businesses / organisations
  • Potentially NHS / commissioned services

Long-term structure

Considering registration as a CIC (Community Interest Company) to access National Lottery and other social-impact funding.

https://lauren.tyneside.software — not live yet. Until then, product truth lives on this docs hub and in the private code repo.
